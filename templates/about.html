ABOUT THE PROJECT

A practical crop reference.

Plant Doctor helps users learn common crop-health topics and prepare better observations for local agricultural support.

What it provides

An India-wide crop disease guide, searchable by crop or condition, with symptoms, immediate checks, prevention and safe next steps.

What it does not do

This site does not use AI, identify photos, collect photos, use an API key, require Google sign-in, track scan history, or impose a daily usage limit.

How to use it

Choose your crop, compare the listed condition with the entire plant, and record visible symptoms, dates and local weather conditions.

Responsible use

For severe, unusual or fast-spreading symptoms, consult a qualified agricultural professional, Krishi Vigyan Kendra or local agriculture office.
